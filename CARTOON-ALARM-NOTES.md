# Cartoon Alarm Overdrive

Version 1.5.0 keeps the v1.4 Emergency Shutdown gameplay and adds a more playful, intense presentation.

## Visual additions

- Silent animated alarm sirens
- Red and amber sweeping light washes
- Comic halftone background texture
- BEEP, GO, DANGER and ZAP comic bursts
- Animated cartoon operator robot
- Chunkier panels, buttons, laptop and keypad feedback
- More energetic Stage 3 warning presentation

All new decorative elements use `pointer-events: none` and cannot cover or intercept keypad input.
